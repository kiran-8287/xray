────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SUBCATEGORY 1.1 — Trolley / Rolling Bags
All bag types with wheels and retractable handles, the most common checked luggage type.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1.  2-wheel upright                    [Classic vertical trolley, single axle rear wheels]
  2.  4-wheel spinner                    [360° rotating wheels, glides in all directions]
  3.  Cabin carry-on trolley             [56×45×25 cm standard airline cabin size]
  4.  Medium check-in trolley            [Typically 65–70 cm height, 20–25 kg capacity]
  5.  Large check-in trolley             [75–80 cm, 28–32 kg capacity]
  6.  Hardshell ABS trolley              [Rigid polycarbonate or ABS shell]
  7.  Hardshell polycarbonate trolley    [Lightweight, high-impact resistance]
  8.  Softshell fabric trolley           [Expandable, external pockets]
  9.  Hybrid trolley                     [Semi-rigid frame with soft panels]
  10. Frame trolley                      [Exposed aluminium/steel frame, vintage style]
  11. Kids' trolley bag                  [Small 16–18 inch, with cartoon prints]
  12. Pilot / crew trolley               [IATA compliant, 45L, flight crew use]
  13. Double decker trolley              [Stacked compartments, tall narrow profile]
  14. Garment trolley bag                [Fold-over design to hang clothes flat]
  15. Business trolley                   [Integrated laptop compartment, slim profile]

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SUBCATEGORY 1.2 — Suitcases

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  3.  Hard-sided suitcase                [ABS/polycarbonate, combination lock]
  4.  Diplomat attaché case              [Flat rectangular hard case, briefcase format]
  5.  Halliburton aluminum case          [Riveted aluminium shell, foam interior]
  6.  Trunk-style suitcase               [Box-shaped, dome lid, often vintage]
  7.  Packing cube set                   [Soft compression cubes inside larger case]
  8.  Nested suitcase set                [3-piece set that fits inside each other]
  9.  Hybrid spinner suitcase            [Spinner wheels + rigid shell]

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  6.  Technical climbing pack            [Ice axe loops, helmet holder]
  7.  Military / tactical backpack       [MOLLE webbing, camo or multicam]
  8.  Hydration backpack                 [Integrated water bladder, tube port]
  9.  Rolling backpack                   [Wheeled base + retractable handle]
  10. Packable ultralight                [Folds into pocket-sized pouch]

  12. Camera backpack                    [Padded dividers, tripod strap]
  13. Baby carrier backpack              [Padded seat for infant, large storage]
  14. Gym / sports backpack              [Ventilated shoe compartment]
  15. Drawstring bag                     [Cinch top, minimal structure]
  16. Harness-style vest pack            [Running vest, chest straps]
  17. Smart backpack                     [USB charging port, GPS tracker]
  18. Bicycle panniers                   [Side-mounted bags, sometimes worn]
